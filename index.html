ABOUT

Trashborn is a PSX-style roguelike action game that takes you back to the golden era of early 2000s gaming. With its low-poly aesthetic, crunchy combat, and procedurally generated dungeons, Trashborn offers a challenging experience reminiscent of classic PlayStation titles.

In a world where everything is disposable, you play as a raccoon fighting through ever-changing wastelands filled with mutated creatures and forgotten technology.

Trashborn has gained a cult following among retro gaming enthusiasts.

FAQ

Q: Will Trashborn work on my modern PC?

A: Yes! While designed to look like a PSX game, Trashborn runs natively on Windows 10/11. We've included compatibility modes if you experience issues.

Q: Is there controller support?

A: Nope.

Q: How difficult is the game?

A: Trashborn is designed to be challenging but fair. Expect to die often as you learn enemy patterns and mechanics. We've included accessibility options to adjust difficulty.

Q: Is there multiplayer?

A: No, Trashborn is a single-player experience.

Q: Will there be more content updates?

A: We're planning at least two major DLC expansions that will add new biomes, enemies, and gameplay mechanics. These will be free for physical edition owners!
